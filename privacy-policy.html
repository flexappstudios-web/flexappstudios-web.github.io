Privacy Policy — Crypto Flex

Effective August 22, 2026 · Flex App Studios

Crypto Flex is a crypto portfolio simulator for entertainment — no real money, no real trading, no real crypto ever changes hands. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Crypto Flex has no server and no account system. Your display name, currency choice, simulated portfolio, and transaction history are stored only in your device's local app storage. We never see this information and it is never transmitted to us.

The app does use two third-party services (described below) that independently collect limited technical data to show ads and process a purchase — that data does not pass through us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Crypto Flex shows optional rewarded video ads via Google AdMob. To serve these ads, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data, and may use this information to personalize ads, subject to your device's ad-tracking settings.

We do not control what AdMob collects. See Google's Partner Sites policy and Google's Privacy Policy for details, and manage your ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com.

In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

Crypto Flex offers an optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase, processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details — Google Play handles the transaction and only tells the app whether the purchase succeeded.

Notifications

If enabled, "come back" reminder notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device using your operating system's built-in notification tools. No notification content is generated by or sent through a remote server.

Children's privacy

Crypto Flex is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app shows ads via Google AdMob, users should be aware of AdMob's own data practices linked above.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling Crypto Flex permanently deletes everything the app stored on your device. We hold no copy of it anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to flexappstudios@gmail.com.
