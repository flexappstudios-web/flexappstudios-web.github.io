The studio

Small studio. Focused products.

Flex App Studios is an independent app development brand created by Bruno Rodrigues and based in Portugal.

What we focus on

We specialize in simulation apps: products that reproduce recognizable digital experiences through carefully designed interfaces, animations, controls and realistic user flows.

Our priority is to make every app intuitive, visually polished and enjoyable to use.

Development principles

  • Realistic and consistent simulation experiences
  • Clean, modern and responsive mobile interfaces
  • Responsible handling of user information
  • Testing across Android devices and screen sizes
  • Ongoing maintenance and improvements
  • Compliance with applicable Google Play policies
Privacy

Privacy & transparency

Flex App Studios respects user privacy. Each application will provide clear information about any data it collects, why that data is required, and how it is handled.

We do not sell personal information. Where third-party services are used, their own privacy terms may also apply. App-specific privacy disclosures will be made available on the relevant Google Play listing and/or a dedicated privacy policy page before release.
